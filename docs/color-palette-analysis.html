Family boundaries are design heuristics, not universal human color categories. Exact greys are separated first; non-grey Oklab lightness below 0.23 goes into “Very dark tints”; low saturation or chroma goes into “Tinted grey”; remaining colors use hue ranges, with dark oranges/yellows grouped as brown/olive. Within each family, the inventory runs from perceptually light to dark using Oklab L.

139 of 256 colors have another color within 0.02 Oklab distance; 216 have one within 0.04. These are descriptive proximity thresholds, not claims of indistinguishability. Display, surroundings, swatch size and color vision affect perception. Method: Oklab by Björn Ottosson.

There is no pure #FFFFFF picture: the lightest exact grey is #FCFCFC. The black picture is 200×201: 40,000 opaque black pixels and 200 transparent pixels. All other color pictures are 200×200 and uniformly opaque. Blue outlines below mark proposed base colors.
